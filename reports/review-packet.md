## Purpose & scope

Fall-prevention agent: predicts dangerous postures from camera pose or IMU streams,
fuses non-motion symptom check-ins, classifies longitudinal risk into stages 0-3, and
emits stage-matched advice. This packet lists every threshold a clinical reviewer
would need to judge, plus current scenario metrics.
